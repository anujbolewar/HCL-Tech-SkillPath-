The Solution • Slide 3 of 10

PathFinder AI: Any Goal. One Clear Path.

An intelligent curriculum architect that transforms natural language goals into interactive Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) roadmaps.

🎯 Personalized Pacing

Dynamically scales module durations to match exact weekly hours (5h, 15h, 30h/week).

🔀 Topological DAG Structure

Guarantees zero circular dependencies. Unlocks downstream milestones as prerequisites are completed.

💡 Explainable AI (XAI)

Deterministic 3-factor relevance scoring (/100) justifying every milestone recommendation.

🤖 Word-by-Word AI Mentor

Grounded in the learner's active roadmap, delivering contextual tutoring and weekly scheduling.

System Architecture • Slide 4 of 10

The 6-Pillar Framework

Pillar 1 & 2: Intake & Profiling

Natural language goal intake + multi-attribute profiling (experience, hours, mastered skills).

Pillar 3 & 4: Recs & DAG Flow

Interactive 2D React Flow canvas with node click inspection and top-tier open providers.

Pillar 5 & 6: XAI & Dashboard

Transparent rationale ledger, streaming mentor, dynamic polar radar chart & 3 export formats.
